# Deep Analysis — Dry Run

Press `⌘⇧K` (macOS) or `Ctrl+Shift+K` to run.

CatalystOps neutralizes your script, runs it on your cluster or serverless compute, and captures the Catalyst physical plan. Results appear in the sidebar with cost estimates and fix suggestions.

**Finds:** broadcast vs sort-merge joins, shuffle bottlenecks, missing partition filters, repeated scans, and more — issues invisible to static analysis.
